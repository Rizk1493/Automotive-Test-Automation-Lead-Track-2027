## Purpose

Track practical job-readiness progress rather than topic exposure.

---

# Mastery Scale

## ORANGE
Needs guidance or has not yet been practically validated at the required level.

## INITIAL GREEN
Can perform a direct task independently.

## MASTERED
Can recall and correctly apply the skill later inside integrated work without being told which skill to use.

A skill is not MASTERED after one easy exercise.

# Validation Rules

## INITIAL GREEN

A direct skill can become INITIAL GREEN when:

1. The concept is understood.
2. A direct task is completed without step-by-step guidance.
3. The result is tested.
4. Relevant edge cases are considered.
5. The solution can be explained.

## MASTERED

A skill becomes MASTERED only when it is later:

1. Recalled without prompting.
2. Applied correctly inside a different integrated problem.
3. Tested appropriately.
4. Used with acceptable code/test quality.
5. Explainable in engineering/interview terms.
